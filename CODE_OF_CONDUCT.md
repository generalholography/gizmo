# Code of Conduct

Gizmo uses a simple contributor covenant: be respectful, constructive, and
focused on making the project better.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat other contributors with patience and professionalism.
- Assume good intent, especially when discussing bugs or design tradeoffs.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and relevant to the work.
- Respect maintainer decisions about project scope and release timing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to move on.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block accounts; or
decline contributions that violate this code of conduct.

If you need to report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately with
links, screenshots, or other relevant context.
